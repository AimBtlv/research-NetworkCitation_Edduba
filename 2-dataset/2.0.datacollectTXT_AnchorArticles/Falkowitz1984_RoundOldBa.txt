Round Old Bab/Ionian School Tablets from Nippur
By Robert S. Falkowitz (Ithaca, N. Y.)
I. Introduction
This article is the first in a series of studies on the practice tablets of the Old Babylonian schools
at Nippur. The information in these studies may be used to complement those earlier studies on the
Old Babylonian schools 1 which were based primarily on the ancient Mesopotamian descriptions
of and allusions to the schools by the scribes and students themselves, and to supplement those
studies which derive information from cuneiform tablets as archaeological artifacts, as well as the
contents of the inscriptions thereon 2.
While many school exercise tablets are known from other sites, predominantly Ur, Susa and
Kish, and many exercise tablets of unknown provenience are extant, Old Babylonian Nippur is the
best documented and, like the Kuyunjik Collection, the corpus will remain a touchstone for compara-
tive studies in the foreseeable future.
