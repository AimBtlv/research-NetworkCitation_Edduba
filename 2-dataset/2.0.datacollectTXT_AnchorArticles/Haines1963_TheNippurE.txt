James E. Knudstad will be in charge of the
expedition. Since he came to the Institute in
1957 as Field Architect, Mr. Knudstad has been
a member of Institute expeditions to Nippur in
Iraq, Ptolemais in Libya, Chogha Mish in
Iran, and Nubia in Egypt. He also took part
in the Archeological Reconnaissance in Turkey
and Iran and the Prehistoric Project in Iran.
During the past winter, he was the Field Direc
tor of an expedition to Nubia in the Sudan.
Next winter he will be returning to Nippur
for the third time, to begin a complete study of
the Ekur—a structure which was truly the
heart of religious life in Mesopotamia.
